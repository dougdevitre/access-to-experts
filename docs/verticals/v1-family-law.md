# V1 — Family Law / Co-Parenting (Flagship Vertical)

The flagship vertical for A2X Phase 0–1. Anchored by CoTrackPro's existing content and user base.

---

## Why Family Law First

1. **Founder expertise.** CoTrackPro is a mature child-centered, trauma-informed co-parenting platform. Content and relationships already exist.
2. **Unmet demand.** Family law is expensive, emotionally charged, and under-served. Pro-se filings are growing. Generic AI is unreliable and sometimes dangerous for this population.
3. **Clear ROI.** Saving a user from hiring an attorney for an $800 consultation has obvious value. Enterprise (EAPs, state bars) have clear buying motions.
4. **Compliance proof.** If A2X can operate safely and credibly in family law — arguably the most sensitive vertical — it can operate anywhere.
5. **Network effects.** Family law practitioners, mediators, and therapists all refer to each other. Wins compound.

## Compliance Posture

### Disclaimers (universal for family-law playbooks)

> This playbook provides educational information about family law in the jurisdiction noted. It is not a substitute for advice from your own attorney. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. The best interests of any children involved are paramount; if children are at risk of harm, contact appropriate authorities immediately.

### Child Safety

Every family law playbook:

- Reviewed for child-centered framing
- Does not enable isolation of children from trusted adults
- Does not sexualize or endanger minors
- Surfaces crisis resources when distress signals detected
- Trauma-informed language and pacing

### UPL Defense

- All legal playbooks authored by attorneys licensed in the playbook's jurisdiction
- Content framed as educational
- Users directed to retain own attorneys for case-specific decisions
- No personalized advice via AI-only channels
- Partnership with Missouri Bar for institutional validation

### Sensitive Populations

- DV survivors: incognito session default, quick-exit button, minimal retention
- Unrepresented litigants: warnings about complexity of their case type; when to seek counsel
- Minors: service not directed at users under 18

## Risks Specific to V1

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| UPL complaint from a state bar | Attorney-authored, clear disclaimers, Missouri Bar partnership |
| User applies content incorrectly and suffers adverse legal outcome | Disclaimers, prompting to consult counsel, review tool by user's own attorney |
| Sensitive content triggers distress in users | Trauma-informed design, crisis resources, pause/resume |
| DV survivor account used by abuser | Incognito defaults, quick exit, safety-planning partnerships |
| Content perceived as biased toward one parent | Balanced authorship, explicit "neutral, child-centered" posture |
| Jurisdictional ambiguity (user in IL, content in MO) | Jurisdiction warnings; refuse to proceed if serious mismatch |
