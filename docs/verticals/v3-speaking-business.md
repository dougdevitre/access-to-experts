# Vertical V3 — Speaking & Consulting Business

## Anchor

**Lois Creamer / Book More Business** — 30+ years coaching professional speakers on positioning, pricing, outbound sales, and fee negotiation. Established methodology, existing client list, NSA credibility.

## Buyer Personas

- **Emerging speaker** — transitioning from subject-matter expert to paid keynote speaker
- **Established speaker** — wants to raise fees, diversify revenue, book more corporate gigs
- **Consultant/coach** — wants to add speaking as a revenue stream
- **Speaker bureau** — licenses playbooks for their roster
- **Association staff** — sources vetted speakers via Access To Experts directory

4. **Fee Negotiation Playbook** — raising fees without losing clients

6. **Outbound Email Sequence** — 5-touch sequence for cold prospects

8. **Testimonial Capture System** — scripts and timing for post-event testimonials
9. **Revenue Diversification Map** — keynote, workshop, consulting, licensing, products
10. **Bureau Relationship Playbook** — working with speaker bureaus productively

- Consumer credits (individual speakers)
- "Book More Business Pro" enterprise license for bureaus and associations

## Cross-Vertical Bridge

Family-law attorneys, trial advocates, and civic-tech practitioners who want to speak at conferences are natural cross-sell targets. Speaking revenue funds their practice.

## Regulatory Considerations

Low regulatory risk vertical. Primary concerns: tax treatment of coaching revenue (1099 vs W-2), state-specific business registration, FTC endorsement guidelines for testimonials.
