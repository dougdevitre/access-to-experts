# V2 — Trial Advocacy (Metasystems Vertical)

Second vertical for A2X, launching in Phase 2. Anchored by the Metasystems methodology (Eric Oliver, NITA 2015).

---

## Why Trial Advocacy Second

- **Existing asset.** Founder holds the Metasystems trial advocacy skill (92-file, 12,803-line distributable product with licensing infrastructure already built).
- **Marquee anchor expert.** Eric Oliver's Metasystems is recognized across trial bar and NITA.
- **Complementary to family law.** Many family law litigators need advocacy skills; cross-sell natural.
- **B2B fit.** Trial advocacy content sells well to law firms, public defenders, trial consulting groups.
- **Defensible content.** Methodology-driven (story-building, jury psychology) does not face the same UPL risk as case-specific legal advice.

---

## Content Themes

1. **Case story construction** — turning facts into a narrative
2. **Jury psychology and decision-maker bias** — how judges/juries actually decide
3. **Voir dire** — jury selection and bias detection
4. **Opening statements** — theme development, primacy, anchoring
5. **Direct and cross examination** — witness preparation, question structure
6. **Closing arguments** — synthesis, calls to action
7. **Focus groups and mock juries** — design, deployment, interpretation
8. **Mediation storytelling** — narrative for mediation contexts
9. **Visual aids and demonstratives** — persuasive visualization
10. **Witness preparation** — cognitive coaching, anxiety management

---

## Target Users

- **Trial attorneys** (plaintiff and defense)
- **Public defenders**
- **Trial consultants**
- **Mediators**

Primary: US federal and state trial practice. Content is largely jurisdiction-neutral for methodology (story-building, jury psychology) with jurisdiction-specific notes for procedural specifics.
