# Go-To-Market

Phased GTM sequence for supply, demand, and distribution.

---

## GTM Principles

1. **Supply before demand** — users won't come without experts; experts won't publish without supply infrastructure.
2. **Anchor before scale** — one anchor institution and a handful of anchor experts before opening self-serve.
3. **Vertical depth before vertical breadth** — dominate family law first, then expand.
4. **Enterprise and consumer in parallel** — do not defer enterprise to after consumer scale; they reinforce each other.
5. **Distribution is strategy** — partnerships with institutions provide lower-CAC distribution than paid acquisition.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Months 0–3)

### Supply

- Recruit 10–15 founding experts via founder's network
- Primary source: Lois247, Metasystems, JTA, Access To ecosystem, personal family-law relationships
- Author 20–30 playbooks in flagship vertical
- Author 5–10 playbooks in each of 2 secondary verticals
- Establish peer review pool (minimum 5 reviewers)

### Demand

- No public acquisition yet
- Waitlist for interested users (via landing page)
- Early-access for existing CoTrackPro users

### Distribution

- Identify 3 target institutional partners for pilot
- Secure 1 Letter of Intent

### Metrics

- Founding experts onboarded: 10+
- Playbooks in review: 30+
- LOI: 1

---

## Phase 1 — Beta (Months 3–9)

### Supply

- Scale to 25 active experts
- Expand playbook catalog to 75–100
- Third vertical live
- Establish expert-referral program (existing experts bring peers)

### Demand

- Closed beta: invite-only from waitlist
- Grow to 1,000 active beta users
- CoTrackPro user base as primary seeder
- NPS and qualitative feedback primary metric

### Distribution

- Launch first institutional pilot (AFCC, state bar, or EAP provider)
- Co-branded launch
- Case study in development

### Channels used

- Founder outreach and network
- Waitlist conversion
- CoTrackPro cross-promotion
- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Selected podcast and conference appearances

### Metrics

- Active experts: 25
- Playbook count: 100
- Beta users: 1,000
- Institutional pilots live: 1
- NPS: >40

---

## Phase 2 — Public Launch (Months 9–18)

### Supply

- Open applications (still with full vetting)
- Scale to 100 active experts
- 300+ playbooks
- Second and third verticals at critical mass
- Institutional content licensing for supply amplification

### Demand

- Public launch with consumer tiers
- First paid acquisition (modest budget, targeted)
- Content marketing (vertical-specific blog, video, podcast)
- SEO for long-tail playbook searches
- Referral program

### Distribution

- 5–10 institutional pilots
- First paid enterprise contract ($25K+)
- CLE/CE partnership with at least one state bar
- White-label pilot with one professional body

### Channels

- Organic search (SEO anchor content)
- Content marketing (blog, podcast, webinars)
- Paid search and LinkedIn (modest)
- PR and media
- Conference presence (AFCC, NSA, legal tech, etc.)
- Enterprise direct sales (founder-led)
- Channel partnerships (JTA, Access To ecosystem)

### Metrics

- Active users: 10,000
- Paid subscribers: 2,500
- Experts: 100
- Enterprise contracts: 3–5
- ARR: $1.5–2M

---

## Phase 3 — Scale & International (Months 18–30)

### Supply

- 500+ certified experts
- 1,500+ playbooks
- 4–5 verticals
- International author recruitment (UK first)
- Institutional anchor in at least 2 verticals

### Demand

- Expand paid acquisition
- International marketing (UK launch)
- Sophisticated content marketing
- Podcast launch

### Distribution

- 20+ institutional partnerships
- 15+ enterprise contracts
- First government contract
- Academic research partnership

### Channels

- Mature mix: SEO, content, paid, PR, events, sales, channel
- Enterprise sales team (small, founder+2)
- International PR and partnerships
- Civic / government procurement

### Metrics

- Active users: 50,000
- Paid subscribers: 15,000
- Experts: 500+
- Enterprise contracts: 15+
- ARR: $12–18M
- Series A closed

## Channel Strategy Detail

### Vertical-Specific GTM

**Family Law:**
- Anchor: CoTrackPro (existing distribution)
- Institutional targets: AFCC, state bars, family law sections
- Events: AFCC annual, state bar family law sections
- Content: custody preparation, pro-se resources, co-parenting

**Trial Advocacy:**
- Anchor: Metasystems (existing licensing relationship)
- Institutional targets: NITA, state bar trial advocacy sections
- Events: NITA programs, state bar advocacy CLEs
- Content: story-building, voir dire, opening/closing preparation

**Speaking Business:**
- Anchor: Lois247 / Book More Business (existing client base)
- Institutional targets: NSA, PSA, international speaking associations
- Events: NSA annual, local NSA chapters
- Content: positioning, pricing, sales

**Civic / Government:**
- Anchor: Access To initiative
- Institutional targets: NLC, ICMA, state government associations
- Events: government technology conferences
- Content: service delivery, compliance, stakeholder engagement

**Workforce:**
- Anchor: MO Jobs, STC Jobs, expungement skills
- Institutional targets: workforce boards, state DOLs
- Events: NAWB, workforce summits
- Content: reentry, WIOA compliance, career transitions

### Enterprise Sales Motion

**Founder-led (Phase 2):**
- Founder does first 5 enterprise deals
- Build repeatable playbook and materials
- Document objections and responses

**Small team (Phase 3):**
- Add enterprise AE and SE
- Territory by vertical initially
- Average contract $50–150K

**Scaled team (Phase 4+):**
- Full org (AE, SE, CSM, AM)
- Vertical specialists
- Channel and partner sales
- Government-specific sales capability

## Partnerships Strategy

Three types of partnerships:

1. **Supply partnerships** — content and author recruitment (see [`../partnerships/institutional-playbook.md`](../partnerships/institutional-playbook.md))
2. **Distribution partnerships** — channel partners (legal tech vendors, HR platforms, case management systems)
3. **Tech partnerships** — integrations (Stripe, Persona, AWS, Anthropic, major CRMs, EHRs)

## Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Supply fails to materialize | Anchor tenancy from founder content; institutional partnerships for bulk supply |
| Demand is noisy / low-intent | Targeted vertical content and SEO; institutional distribution lowers noise |
| Enterprise sales cycle too long | Start with mid-market; use state bars for faster revenue |
| Funding gap before Series A | Strategic partner investment; revenue-based financing; grants |
| Competitive response from incumbents | Vertical depth and jurisdiction infrastructure are hard to copy |
