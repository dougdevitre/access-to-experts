# Pricing Tiers

Consumer, enterprise, and institutional pricing for Access To Experts. See [`../unit-economics.md`](../unit-economics.md) for the underlying math.

**Status:** Working pricing; subject to validation in Phase 1 pilots.

---

## Consumer Tiers

### Free Preview

- **$0**
- Browse and preview playbooks (first 3 steps of any playbook)
- Search, filter, expert profiles
- Create account, save favorites
- Cannot start a session beyond preview

### Explorer — $19/month

- 100 credits/month
- Access to full Marketplace
- Session pause/resume
- Artifact downloads
- Basic email support

### Builder — $49/month

- 400 credits/month
- Credit rollover (up to 200)
- Session fork and share
- Priority customer support
- Early access to new verticals

### Operator — $149/month

- 1,200 credits/month
- Credit rollover (up to 600)
- Premium AI model allocation (Opus-class)
- Multi-session parallelism
- API access (limited rate)
- Team-of-1 analytics

### Annual plans

- 15% discount for annual commitment on any tier
- Payment annual, billed monthly or upfront

### One-time credit packs

For users who prefer pay-as-you-go or need a top-up:

| Pack | Credits | Price | Effective $/credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top-up 100 | 100 | $17 | $0.170 |
| Top-up 500 | 500 | $75 | $0.150 |
| Top-up 2,000 | 2,000 | $260 | $0.130 |
| Top-up 10,000 | 10,000 | $1,100 | $0.110 |

Top-up credits do not expire.

## Enterprise Tiers

### Starter (Team) — $500–2,000/month

- Small team or department
- 5–25 seats
- Shared credit pool (50K–200K credits/month)
- Admin console
- Basic SSO (Google)
- Curated library
- Standard SLA

### Professional (Business) — $2,000–10,000/month

- Mid-market
- 25–150 seats
- Shared or per-seat credit allotment
- SAML SSO + SCIM
- Custom library + org branding
- Usage analytics + export
- Priority support
- Dedicated account manager

### Premier (Corporate) — $10,000–50,000/month

- Enterprise
- 150–2,000 seats
- Custom credit contracts
- Advanced SSO (OIDC, SAML)
- Data residency controls
- Audit log export
- Dedicated success manager
- 99.9% SLA
- Custom playbook development (add-on)

### Custom (Global Enterprise) — $50,000+/month

- Multi-national or high-sensitivity enterprise
- 2,000+ seats
- Dedicated tenant (optional)
- CMEK (customer-managed encryption keys)
- Custom SOC 2 scoped reports
- On-premise integrations
- 99.99% SLA available
- Named legal contact

## Government Tiers

Government contracts priced via procurement, not catalog, but reference tiers:

### State / Municipal

- $25K–$500K annual contracts
- Per-seat or pooled credit
- StateRAMP-aligned (Phase 3+)
- US data residency
- Specific state or agency jurisdiction coverage

### Federal (US)

- $250K–$5M annual
- FedRAMP Moderate (Phase 4 target)
- US citizen data handling
- GSA schedule or dedicated contract vehicle

### International Government

- Per-country procurement
- Local data residency
- Local language and jurisdiction adaptation
- Multi-year commitment typical

---

## White-Label / OEM

Professional associations, EAP providers, bar associations, etc.

### Setup

- One-time setup fee: $25K–$100K
- Custom branding, domain, SSO integration
- Custom library curation
- Member onboarding flow

### Ongoing

- Revenue share model (typical 70/30 favor of partner, 60/40, or negotiated)
- OR flat per-member fee
- OR flat platform fee + per-use credits
- Annual minimums apply

## Credentialing / CLE Partnerships

- Partner (regulatory body): no upfront
- Per-credit fee: $2–$10 per accredited credit earned (A2X share)
- Practitioner pays: $10–$40 per credit-hour (market rate)

## Discounts & Programs

### Founding users

- Early adopters of Phase 1 beta: 50% off for 12 months

### Nonprofits

- 30% off Explorer/Builder/Operator with 501(c)(3) verification
- Bespoke pricing for nonprofit enterprise

### Pro bono / public defenders

- Explorer-tier free for verified public defenders and legal aid orgs
- Scaled up via civic-tech grant funding

### Students / academics

- 50% off Explorer with .edu verification

### Missouri civic anchor

- State of Missouri gets anchor pricing as part of civic-tech partnership
- Community colleges and libraries get free Explorer seats under partnership

### Referral program

- Users earn bonus credits for successful referrals
- Experts earn bonus revenue share for referring users

## Payment Methods

- **Consumer:** credit card (Stripe), Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Enterprise:** invoicing (NET-30), ACH, credit card
- **Government:** purchase orders, invoicing per contract
- **International:** local payment methods added per market (SEPA, BACS, Interac, etc.)

---

## Contracts

- **Consumer:** click-through ToS; monthly or annual commitment
- **Enterprise:** signed MSA + order form; typically 1–3 year
- **Government:** per government contract template
- **White-label:** signed partnership agreement with revenue terms

---

## Pricing Governance

- Consumer price changes: 30-day notice for existing subscribers; new price applies at next renewal
- Enterprise price changes: per contract terms (typically renewal only)
- Grandfathering available for founding members
- No retroactive price increases

---

## Pricing Review Cadence

- **Quarterly:** consumer tier usage and conversion review
- **Semi-annually:** enterprise pricing benchmark vs. competitors
- **Annually:** full pricing model review with board input
