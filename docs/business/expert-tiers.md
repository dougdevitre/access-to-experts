# Expert Tiers

Three tiers of expert on the A2X platform, with different expectations, economics, and promotion paths.

---

## Tier Overview

| Tier | Who | Economics | Visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anchor** | Globally recognized, co-branded, prestige | 60/40 or custom, often with guarantees, possible equity | Top-placement; marketing features |
| **Certified** | Credentialed professionals, QA-reviewed | 70/30 standard | Standard ranking, merit-based |
| **Emerging** | Newer or specialized practitioners on probation | 70/30, reduced visibility | Long-tail; performance-based promotion |

---

## Anchor Tier

### Criteria

- Nationally or internationally recognized
- Criteria examples:
  - Named partner at a top-50 law firm
  - Board-certified specialist in their field
  - Bestselling author (traditional publication)
  - Former high-level government position (federal judge, state AG, medical examiner general, etc.)
  - Named professor or academic chair at R1/major institution
  - Major award recipient in their vertical
  - High-profile speaker with >$25K/talk fees
- Strategic fit with A2X's vertical roadmap
- Content potential (10+ playbooks or one flagship curriculum)
- Co-marketing willingness

### Responsibilities

- Author signature playbook series (typically 5–15)
- Participate in launch PR and case studies
- Host quarterly "Anchor Talk" (optional; paid separately)
- Advise on vertical strategy (optional Advisory Council seat)
- Represent vertical in institutional partnerships

### Economics

- Custom revenue split (60/40 typical; negotiated)
- Annual revenue guarantee (varies, typically $50K–$250K)
- Marketing placement premium
- Possible platform equity (advisor shares)
- Priority on enterprise inclusions
- First-look on new vertical partnerships

### Onboarding

- Direct executive relationship (founder or CEO in Phase 1–2; vertical lead Phase 3+)
- White-glove playbook authoring support (editorial, AI prompt engineering, template design)
- Dedicated success manager
- Co-branded launch

### Examples of candidate anchors

- Family law: Jill Duffy (Missouri), Gary Neustadter (California), Milfred Dale (AFCC)
- Trial advocacy: Eric Oliver (Metasystems) — confirmed ecosystem relationship
- Speaking business: Lois Creamer (Book More Business) — confirmed ecosystem relationship

- Civic: (to be recruited with JTA and Access To networks)

## Certified Tier

### Criteria

- Active license/credential in their practice area
- Minimum experience threshold (3 years post-credential typical; varies by vertical)
- Professional liability insurance (where required)
- Clean record (no open disciplinary actions)
- Demonstrated expertise (portfolio, writing samples, references)
- Pass content QA on first published playbook

### Responsibilities

- Author and maintain at least one published playbook
- Respond to flagged content within 10 business days
- Keep credentials current
- Pass annual re-verification
- Follow community guidelines

### Economics

- 70/30 revenue split (expert/platform)
- Opt-in premium pricing for high-demand content
- Monthly payout schedule
- Standard analytics dashboard

### Onboarding

- Self-serve application
- Identity and credential verification (Persona + API lookup)
- Authoring guide + visual builder
- Peer review required before first publication
- 30–60 day typical onboarding time

### Promotion to Anchor

Certified experts can be invited to Anchor tier based on:

- Sustained high performance (rating, outcomes, completions)
- Strategic content coverage (fills a platform need)
- Audience build on platform
- Recognition acquired while on platform (books, awards, speaking)

No guaranteed promotion path; Anchor tier is scarce and strategic.

---

## Emerging Tier

### Criteria

- Meets minimum credential requirements
- Less experienced or newly credentialed
- Entering a new practice area
- Accepted after basic verification + application review

### Responsibilities

- Author playbooks with peer support
- Accept more intensive peer review during probation
- Limited initial jurisdictions/practice areas
- Responsive to feedback

### Economics

- 70/30 revenue split
- Reduced marketplace visibility during probation
- No marketing placement
- Standard payout schedule

### Onboarding

- Self-serve application
- Identity and credential verification
- First playbook heavily peer-reviewed
- Lower credit price ceiling during probation

### Promotion to Certified

Automatic review at:

- 6 months on platform
- 25+ completions across all playbooks
- 4.3+ average rating
- At least one peer-reviewed and approved playbook
- No outstanding content flags

If criteria met → auto-promote with notification. If not met → feedback + continued Emerging status with path forward.

### Exit from Emerging

- Promotion to Certified, OR
- Voluntary departure, OR
- Involuntary removal for policy violations or inability to pass QA

## Cross-Tier Considerations

### Peer Review Participation

- **Anchor:** can be requested for peer review; compensated per review
- **Certified:** expected to participate in peer review (paid modestly per review)
- **Emerging:** cannot peer-review until promoted to Certified

### Content Takedown & Appeals

All tiers have the right to appeal content decisions. Anchor tier disputes have direct line to founding team; Certified and Emerging go through standard review.

### Deplatforming

Any tier can be deplatformed for:

- Loss of underlying credential
- Serious policy violation
- Fraud or misrepresentation
- Repeated user harm
- Failure to maintain insurance (if required)

Anchor deplatforming requires Ethics Board review (given likely high public profile). Certified/Emerging follows standard moderation process.

### Tax & Payments

- US experts: W-9 required at signup; 1099 issued annually when earnings meet the IRS reporting threshold ($2,000 starting with payments made in 2026; it was $600 through 2025)
- International experts: appropriate forms per country; tax treaty handling via Stripe Connect

## Expert Council Representation

The Expert Council (see [`../../GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md)) is elected by active Certified and Anchor experts. Representation:

- Vertical-balanced seats
- 2-year terms
- Elections annually (staggered)

Emerging experts do not vote in Council elections (eligibility begins at Certified tier).

## Incentives Across Tiers

### Content velocity incentives

- Bonuses for publishing in under-served jurisdictions
- Bonuses for under-served practice areas (platform-identified gaps)
- Referral incentives for inviting peer experts to platform

### Quality incentives

- Rating-based revenue share accelerators (Certified tier experts consistently rated >4.6 earn extra 5% for 90-day windows)
- Outcome-based bonuses for documented successful completions in regulated verticals (pilot Phase 2)

### Growth incentives

- Marketing credits for anchor experts
- Free enterprise previews for corporate clients they refer
- Speaker fee opportunities at A2X events
