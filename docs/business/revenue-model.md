# Revenue Model

Target revenue composition, strategic rationale, and evolution.

---

## Target Mix at Steady State (Year 3+)

| Segment | % | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise & Government | 40% | High-ticket, multi-year, high NRR; anchors cash flow |
| Consumer (Subscription + Credits) | 25% | High volume, signal-dense, supports supply engagement |
| Institutional Licensing / White-Label | 20% | Low-CAC distribution; compounding credibility |
| API & Integrations | 10% | Developer ecosystem moat |
| Credentialing Fees | 5% | Regulatory partnership; unlocks enterprise |

---

## Why This Mix

### 40% Enterprise

- Multi-year contracts are balance-sheet assets
- Enterprise NRR (targeted >110%) drives durable growth
- Less sensitive to consumer marketing noise
- Funds R&D and international expansion
- Reduces reliance on consumer acquisition costs

### 25% Consumer

- Large TAM
- Signal and learning (what works, what doesn't)
- Supply engagement (experts need consumer activity to justify effort)
- Brand and word-of-mouth

### 20% Institutional

- Pre-built distribution (bar associations, professional bodies)
- Low-CAC
- Credibility multiplier
- Competitive moat (institutions rarely switch providers)

### 10% API

- High-margin
- Ecosystem lock-in
- Strategic partnerships (legal tech vendors, HR platforms)

### 5% Credentialing

- Unlocks CLE/CE/CME
- Strategic for regulatory goodwill
- Small but sticky

---

## Revenue Evolution

### Year 1

- 100% consumer-led (mostly founders' network)
- Some pilot institutional revenue ($25K)
- Total: ~$120K

### Year 2

- 75% consumer, 20% enterprise (3–5 contracts), 5% institutional
- Total: ~$1.7M ARR

### Year 3

- 55% consumer, 30% enterprise, 10% institutional, 5% API
- Total: ~$15M ARR

### Year 5

- 25% consumer, 40% enterprise, 20% institutional, 10% API, 5% credentialing/data
- Total: ~$95–100M ARR

### Year 10 (aspirational)

- 20% consumer, 45% enterprise, 20% institutional, 10% API, 5% data products
- Data products could be larger share by Year 7+ depending on Phase 4 execution
- Total: $500M+

---

## Margin Profile

- **Consumer gross margin:** ~65–70% (after expert share, AI, infra)
- **Enterprise gross margin:** ~70–80% (volume and contract terms)
- **Institutional gross margin:** varies by deal (40–75%)
- **API gross margin:** ~85%+ (minimal marginal cost)
- **Data products gross margin (Phase 3+):** 85–90%

Blended gross margin target: 70%+ by Year 3.

## Key Revenue Drivers

### Supply drivers

- Expert count and tier distribution
- Playbook catalog depth per vertical
- Jurisdiction coverage
- Content freshness
- Peer review throughput

### Demand drivers

- Search conversion (visit → session)
- Session completion rate
- NPS and referrals
- Enterprise logo growth
- Enterprise NRR
- Institutional partnership count

### Economic drivers

- Credit price
- Expert revenue share
- AI inference cost per session
- CAC per segment
- LTV per segment

---

## Concentration Risk

- **No single enterprise customer >15% of revenue** (target)
- **No single expert >5% of revenue** (target)
- **No single vertical >40% of revenue** (target at steady state; early phases have family law anchor concentration)

Risk mitigation:

- Diversify customer base via segment and vertical
- Diversify expert supply across anchors
- International expansion reduces single-country risk

---

## Pricing Power Levers

- Vertical specialization commands premium (legal > generic)
- Jurisdiction completeness commands premium
- Anchor expert association commands premium
- Enterprise features (SSO, compliance, SLAs) command premium
- Data residency and tenancy options (Phase 3+) command premium
