# Vision

## Thesis

The world's most valuable expertise is trapped inside individuals, firms, and institutions. Access to a top family law attorney, a Harvard-trained negotiator, a Mayo Clinic specialist, or a former Fortune 500 CHRO depends on geography, network, and budget. For most people, that access is unavailable, unaffordable, or unreliable.

The alternatives available today are poor substitutes:

- **Generic AI chatbots** produce confident but unaccountable output
- **Template libraries** are static and offer no guidance
- **Legal tech and health tech tools** are narrow and transactional
- **Hiring an expert** is expensive, slow, and limited by availability

There is no system that lets a credentialed practitioner package their exact methodology as a structured, executable workflow that others can run with AI guidance, producing real artifacts with real accountability.

**Access To Experts (A2X) is that system.**

## What A2X Is

A2X is a world-class expert-led platform — a system of record where the world's top practitioners package their methodology as guided, executable playbooks. Users execute those playbooks through an AI-guided three-pane interface, generating artifacts, decisions, and plans at a fraction of the cost of 1:1 expert engagement.

A2X sits at the intersection of four existing categories:

- **Marketplace** (Upwork, Fiverr): expert access, but fragmented and unstructured
- **Education** (Coursera, MasterClass, Maven): expertise transfer, but knowledge-only not execution
- **Workflow tools** (TurboTax, Rocket Lawyer): guided execution, but narrow verticals and static content
- **Professional services** (law firms, consultancies): deep expertise, but unscalable and expensive

A2X combines the access of a marketplace, the depth of professional services, the structure of workflow tools, and the scalability of software.

## The Operating Model

### Three-Pane Interface

A2X is delivered through a three-pane user interface:

- **Guide (left)** — AI assistant helps the user scope their need and suggests playbooks
- **Marketplace (center)** — curated results with expert credentials, outcomes, and previews
- **Workspace (right)** — active session with step-by-step execution, artifact generation, and saved history

This pattern mirrors the TripAdvisor mental model: discovery + execution + confidence in one workflow.

### Credit Metering

Usage is metered in credits. Credits are consumed per step, per AI turn, and per artifact generation. Users replenish credits via subscription tiers (Explorer, Builder, Operator) or one-time purchase. Enterprises purchase credit bundles or seat-based licenses.

### Three-Tier Expert Stack

- **Anchor Experts** — globally recognized, co-branded, prestige content with revenue guarantees
- **Certified Practitioners** — credentialed, QA-reviewed, standard revenue share (70% expert / 30% platform)
- **Emerging Experts** — long-tail coverage with performance-based promotion

### Vertical Organization

A2X is organized by vertical, each with its own taxonomy, compliance posture, and anchor institutions:

1. Legal (family law, trial advocacy, business law, immigration)
2. Clinical (mental health, trauma-informed care, eventual medical)
3. Business (strategy, HR, sales, marketing, finance)
4. Civic (government, housing, workforce, education)
5. Specialty (speaking business, coaching, consulting, trades)

## The Moat

A2X is defensible in ways that generic AI, template libraries, and consulting firms are not:

### Institutional Supply

The platform anchors with institutions — bar associations, medical societies, professional bodies — not just individuals. These partnerships bring credibility, content, and distribution in a single package.

### Jurisdiction-Native Architecture

A2X treats jurisdiction as a first-class data primitive. Every playbook declares its scope (country, state, county). The platform enforces jurisdiction awareness at execution time. Competitors retrofitting this face a multi-year engineering burden.

### Outcome Data

Every session produces structured outcome data. Aggregated over millions of executions, this becomes a proprietary data asset:

- Outcome benchmarking ("Playbooks for X have a 78% success rate in MO")
- Expert performance analytics (objective, outcome-based)
- Regulatory intelligence (flagging playbooks affected by statute changes)
- Training data for AI labs (consented, structured, expert-validated)

This is the same moat Bloomberg, Westlaw, and PitchBook built. The platform is a feature; the data is the business.

### Trust Infrastructure

A2X invests in enterprise-grade trust from day one:

- SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA readiness
- Verified expert credentials with continuous monitoring
- Peer-reviewed content with Ethics Board oversight
- Clear liability framework with insurance coverage
- Transparent governance with independent boards

Competitors cannot match this posture without a multi-year rebuild.

### Ecosystem Network Effects

A2X launches inside an existing ecosystem of related platforms (CoTrackPro, Lois247, Metasystems, JTA, Access To initiative, MO Startups, MO Jobs). Each generates playbook content, expert relationships, and distribution channels that make A2X more valuable and harder to replicate.

## The Economics

### Consumer Tier (Phase 1–2)

- Explorer: $19/mo, 100 credits
- Builder: $49/mo, 400 credits
- Operator: $99–$149/mo, 1,000+ credits
- One-time credit purchases available

**Target:** 25% of revenue mix at steady state.

### Enterprise Tier (Phase 2+)

- Corporate Licenses: $10K–$500K/yr (seat-based)
- Government Contracts: $50K–$5M (multi-year)
- White-Label: revenue share + setup fees
- API Access: usage-based

**Target:** 40% of revenue mix at steady state.

### Institutional & Credentialing (Phase 2+)

- Professional body licensing
- CLE/CE/CME partnerships
- Academic research licensing

**Target:** 20% of revenue mix at steady state.

### Data & Ecosystem (Phase 3+)

- Outcome benchmarking products
- Training data licensing
- Developer API marketplace

**Target:** 15% of revenue mix at steady state.

### Unit Economics

- Average credit cost to user: $0.15
- Average revenue per playbook completion: $6.00
- Expert share (70%): $4.20
- AI/compute cost per completion: $0.35
- Stripe + infra: $0.30
- Platform contribution margin: ~19%

At 100,000 monthly completions: ~$600K monthly revenue, ~$115K contribution. Subscription revenue funds operations; enterprise revenue funds growth.

## Why Now

Three convergent trends make A2X possible in 2026 and not before:

1. **AI reliability crossed the threshold.** Claude and other frontier models can now execute multi-step professional workflows reliably when grounded in structured content and expert-authored playbooks.

2. **Expert professions face margin pressure.** Solo practitioners and small firms are increasingly unable to compete with AI-augmented incumbents. They need a platform that lets them monetize their methodology, not just their time.

3. **Trust is a scarce resource.** Generic AI has produced widespread skepticism. Users are actively seeking provenance, credentials, and accountability — which A2X provides by design.

## The Commitment

A2X will be built with these non-negotiables:

- **Expert integrity over growth metrics.** A bad playbook damages every other expert. Quality gates are inviolable.
- **User safety over completion rates.** Playbooks that could harm users (UPL risk, unsafe medical advice) are refused regardless of demand.
- **Jurisdiction honesty.** No playbook claims applicability beyond what its author can credentialize.
- **Transparent economics.** Experts always know what they earn and why. Users always know what they paid for.
- **Child-centered and trauma-informed.** Where the platform touches vulnerable populations, their interests are paramount.
